Chapter Two

Meet the Creator

Victoria Tinch

Girl Scout Gold Award Recipient • Student Leader • Engineering and Technology Advocate

Victoria created the original Staying Safe Online! Cybersecurity Curriculum to help young people understand the risks they may encounter while using technology.

Her work combined research, classroom lessons, interactive activities, student discussions, take-home resources, and support materials for parents and educators.

Safetii Net continues that mission through stories, missions, games, badges, and challenges.

Chapter Three

Five Missions Build One Cyber Hero

Scroll through the mission path and discover the skills students practice along the way.

Mission 1

Identity Protector

Learn what information is personal, private, secret, or safe to share.

Mission 2

Password Safe Keeper

Build stronger passwords and add protection to important accounts.

Mission 3

Phish Finder

Investigate suspicious links, requests, messages, and online offers.

Mission 4

Digital Footprint Defender

Understand how online choices can shape a lasting digital reputation.

Mission 5

Cyber Responder

Practice blocking, reporting, saving evidence, and asking for help.

Chapter Four

The Safetii Net Promise

🎮

Learning Should Be Engaging

Students learn through missions, scenarios, games, choices, feedback, and discovery.

🧠

Lessons Should Be Understandable

Cybersecurity ideas use clear language, relatable examples, and age-appropriate situations.

🤝

Trusted Adults Matter

Students are encouraged to communicate openly and seek help when something feels unsafe.

🌱

Skills Should Last

Safetii Net teaches habits students can continue using as technology changes.
