The Story Behind Safetii Net

Helping Young People Become Safer Digital Citizens

Safetii Net is an interactive cybersecurity learning experience created to help elementary and middle school students recognize online risks, protect their personal information, and make safer choices in the digital world.

Why It Was Created

The Project Story

01

Recognizing the Need

Children begin using technology at an early age, but many do not receive enough instruction about scams, unsafe sharing, identity risks, phishing, privacy, or the long-term effects of online activity.

02

Making Learning Engaging

Victoria created lessons, presentations, games, discussions, quizzes, hands-on activities, and family resources so cybersecurity education would feel approachable rather than overwhelming.

03

Expanding the Impact

Safetii Net turns the original curriculum into an interactive online experience that can continue helping students, families, and educators beyond the original workshops.

Cybersecurity Skills

What Students Learn

Each Safetii Net mission turns an important digital safety topic into a guided challenge.

🪪

Protecting Identity

Recognizing personal, private, and secret information and understanding when information should not be shared.

🔐

Password Safety

Creating stronger passwords, understanding password attacks, using unique passwords, and adding two-factor protection.

🎣

Spotting Phishing

Identifying suspicious links, fake messages, urgent requests, unbelievable offers, and other scam warning signs.

👣

Digital Footprints

Understanding that online activity leaves a lasting record and learning to think carefully before posting.

🛡️

Responding Safely

Knowing when to stop, save evidence, block or report an account, and ask a trusted adult for support.

💬

Responsible Online Behavior

Treating others respectfully, protecting privacy, and making choices that support a positive digital future.

About the Creator

Victoria Tinch

Girl Scout Gold Award Recipient • Student Leader • Engineering and Technology Advocate

Victoria created the original Staying Safe Online! Cybersecurity Curriculum to help young people understand the risks they may encounter while using technology.

Her work combined cybersecurity research, classroom lessons, interactive activities, student discussions, take-home resources, and support materials for parents and educators.

Safetii Net continues that mission by transforming the curriculum into an online learning world where students can practice cybersecurity skills through stories, missions, games, badges, and challenges.

Our Learning Approach

The Safetii Net Promise

🎮

Learning Should Be Engaging

Students learn through missions, scenarios, games, choices, feedback, and discovery.

🧠

Lessons Should Be Understandable

Cybersecurity ideas are explained using clear language, relatable examples, and age-appropriate situations.

🤝

Trusted Adults Matter

Students are encouraged to communicate openly and seek help when an online situation feels unsafe.

🌱

Skills Should Last

Safetii Net teaches habits students can use as technology, platforms, and online risks continue to change.
