Mission 3

Phish Finder

Can you outsmart a scammer?

Step 1: Learn

Phishing is a type of cyberattack where malicious actors attempt to trick individuals into revealing sensitive information.

Phishers may use fake emails, text messages, websites, or QR codes to trick you.

Step 2: Fishy Signs of a Scam

  • Weird spelling or grammar.
  • Strange links.
  • Messages that sound too good to be true.
  • Urgent messages like “CLICK NOW!”

Step 3: Practice

“Free Robux! Click NOW to claim!”
