What is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of cyberattack where malicious actors try to trick people into revealing sensitive information.

In other words, phishing is when someone sends a fake message to trick you into sharing private information, clicking a bad link, or giving away your password.

🔒 Passwords 🔒 Addresses 🔒 Money 🔒 Account codes 🔒 Private information 🔒 Identification Cards 🔒 Social Security

Why is phishing Dangerous?

Phishers use fake emails, text messages, QR codes, or websites that look real.

They may pretend to be your favorite game, app, store, school, or friend to trick you into giving them passwords, addresses, information, or money.

Facing the Phishers

Fishy signs of a scam

1. Asking for Private Info

Scammers may ask for passwords, addresses, usernames, or account codes.

2. Strange Links

Example: “www.robux-free-prize.biz”

3. Too Good to Be True

Example: “Free iPhone if you click NOW!”

Anatomy of a Phishing Email

From: service@paypa1-support.example

Subject: Response required

Dear customer,

We noticed your account has been limited. You must log in right away or your account will be removed.

Click here to fix your account: www.paypal-security-check.example

False urgency Sender looks strange Vague wording Suspicious link Generic greeting Incorrect spelling

Quishing

Quishing is a cyberattack that uses malicious QR codes to trick people into visiting fake websites, downloading malware, or revealing sensitive data.

Before scanning a QR code, ask: Who made this? Where does it go? Do I trust it?

Scan me? Wait first!

Thinking Moment

What to do if You Find a Scam

STOP

Do not click on sketchy links.

BLOCK

Do not allow them to message you again.

TELL

Tell an adult or report the account.
