      Build two layers of account protection by combining something the user knows with something the user has or something the user is.

      One lock is helpful. Two different locks are stronger.

      A password is usually something you know. Two-factor authentication adds a different kind of proof, such as something you have or something you are.

      A password, passphrase, or PIN.

      📱

      Something You Have

      A trusted device, changing authenticator code, or physical security key.

      🫆

      Something You Are
