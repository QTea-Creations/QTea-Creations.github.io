Identity Protector

Your mission is to learn how to protect your personal information online.

Step 1: Learn

Personal information is anything that can identify you, contact you, or show where you are. This can include your full name, address, school, phone number, passwords, and location.

Step 2: Practice

Decide if this is safe or not safe:

You tell an online friend your full name, age, and school.

Step 3: Quiz

What should you do if someone online asks for private information?

Step 4: Badge

Complete the practice and quiz to unlock your Identity Protector badge.
