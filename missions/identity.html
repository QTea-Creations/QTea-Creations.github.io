What is private information?

Private information is anything that could help someone find you, contact you, pretend to be you, or get into your accounts.

🏠

Keep Private

Your address, school name, phone number, passwords, and full name.

🍕

Usually Safe

Your favorite pizza, favorite color, hobbies, or favorite animal.

🦸

Use a Hero Name

A fun nickname is safer than using your real first and last name.
