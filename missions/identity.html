Mission 1

Identity Protector

Learn how to keep your personal information safe online.

Step 1: Learn

Sensitive information is personal or private data that, if misused or revealed, could cause harm to yourself or family.

Examples include your name, address, passwords, school, phone number, and location.

Step 2: Why It Matters

  • Someone could pretend to be you.
  • Your information could be stolen.
  • Strangers could learn where you live or go to school.
  • Your accounts or money could be at risk.

Step 3: Practice — Safe or Not Safe?

You tell your online friend your full name, your age, and the name of your school.
