Hero Briefing

Choose the Safest Audience

🌍

Anyone Online

Broad interests and harmless information that would not identify, locate, or endanger someone.

Example: “I enjoy drawing cartoons.”
🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Friends I Know

Personal details that should only be shared with real friends—not strangers met online.

Example: “I’m nervous about tomorrow’s test.”
👨‍👩‍👧

Trusted Adults

Information that a parent, guardian, teacher, counselor, or another safe adult may need to know.

Example: “Someone online is threatening me.”
🔒

Keep It Private

Passwords, verification codes, home addresses, live locations, PINs, and account access details.

Example: “My password is GalaxyCat82.”
🎯

Read the entire situation before choosing. The safest audience may change depending on what is happening.
