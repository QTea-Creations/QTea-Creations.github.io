Lab Briefing

Build a Safer Username

🎨

Use Interests

Hobbies, animals, colors, fictional ideas, and creative words are usually safer choices.

Example: CosmicPanda
🚫

Skip Full Names

Avoid using first and last names together, especially on public accounts.

Avoid: MayaJohnson
🎂

Hide Birth Information

Birth years, exact ages, and birthdays can help strangers guess someone’s identity.

Avoid: Tyler2014
🏫

Protect Locations

Do not include schools, teams, cities, streets, neighborhoods, or house numbers.

Avoid: EastRidgeEagle24
🎯

Judge the whole username. Several harmless-looking clues can combine to identify someone.

🔬
