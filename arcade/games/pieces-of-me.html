How to Play

Know the Four Categories

🎨

Safe Self-Expression

Hobbies, favorite subjects, talents, interests, and broad preferences.

Example: “I love drawing.”
👤

Personal Information

General facts that deserve care but do not directly identify or locate someone.

Example: “I have two siblings.”
🔒

Private Information

Grade, age, birthday, school, address, neighborhood, location, or schedule.

Example: “I attend Westview Academy.”
🔑

Secret Information

Passwords, PINs, verification codes, and account recovery information.

Example: “My login code is 482991.”
