Password Defense Notes

  • Long passwords and passphrases are generally harder to guess than short passwords.
  • Common words, personal clues, keyboard patterns, dates, and number sequences weaken passwords.
  • A dictionary attack checks common words and password combinations.
  • A brute-force attack tries many possible combinations.
  • Every important account should use a different password.
  • Two-factor authentication combines two different types of proof.
  • Verification codes should never be sent to someone who asks for them.
  • Suspicious account activity should be checked through the official app or website with help from a trusted adult.
